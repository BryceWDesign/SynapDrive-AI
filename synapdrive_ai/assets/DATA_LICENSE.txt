Contains information from the EEG Motor Movement/Imagery Dataset v1.0.0,
Gerwin Schalk (2009), PhysioNet, https://doi.org/10.13026/C28G6P ,
https://physionet.org/content/eegmmidb/1.0.0/ , made available under the
Open Data Commons Attribution License (ODC-By) v1.0:
https://physionet.org/content/eegmmidb/view-license/1.0.0/
https://opendatacommons.org/licenses/by/1-0/

This transformed subset selects S001-S008, runs 04/08/12, nine channels,
one 0.5-3.5s epoch per eligible event, float32 values in volts, and adds explicit
subject/run/trial arrays. The source data retain their separate database license;
the repository code's Apache-2.0 license does not replace those terms.

See docs/RECORDED_EEG.md for attribution, transformation and reproduction details.
