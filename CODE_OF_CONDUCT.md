# Code of Conduct

SynapDrive-AI discussions should remain professional, technical, and respectful.

## Expected behavior

- Critique ideas, evidence, code, and claims rather than people.
- Keep technical disagreements specific and reproducible when possible.
- Respect privacy and do not publish personal, participant, credential, or device data without authorization.
- Correct inaccurate claims when evidence changes.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, personal attacks, or sustained disruption are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove content or restrict participation when behavior violates this policy.
